GDPR Compliance & Data Protection

Last Updated: November 2024

GDPR • UK GDPR • ePrivacy Directive Compliant

1. GDPR Compliance Overview

AVIJO TASK PRO complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR. This document outlines how we comply with GDPR requirements.

2. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): When you explicitly opt-in to cloud sync features
  • Contract (Article 6(1)(b)): To provide the Service as requested
  • Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): When required by law
  • Legitimate Interest (Article 6(1)(f)): Security, fraud prevention, and service improvement

3. Data Subject Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

Right to Access (Article 15)

You have the right to request access to your personal data. We will provide you with a copy within 30 days.

Right to Rectification (Article 16)

You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

Right to Erasure (Article 17)

You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal limitations. We will delete data within 30 days of your request.

Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18)

You can request that we limit how we use your data while we address your concerns.

Right to Data Portability (Article 20)

You can request your data in a machine-readable format and transfer it to another service.

Right to Object (Article 21)

You can object to certain processing of your data, particularly marketing and profiling.

Rights Related to Automated Decision Making (Article 22)

AVIJO TASK PRO does not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects.

4. Data Processing Agreement

For businesses processing personal data on behalf of AVIJO TASK PRO, a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available upon request. Contact: legal@avojotask.com

5. Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

AVIJO TASK PRO conducts DPIAs for processing activities that pose high risks to data subjects' rights and freedoms. We document our findings and implement protective measures.

6. International Transfers

If personal data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, AVIJO TASK PRO ensures:

  • Adequate safeguards are in place (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses)
  • Compliance with Chapter V of GDPR
  • Appropriate technical and organizational measures

7. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Retained until you delete it
  • Deleted Items: Retained in Bin for 30 days
  • Cloud Sync Data: Retained per your account settings
  • Account Deletion: All associated data deleted within 30 days

8. Data Protection Officer (DPO)

AVIJO TASK PRO has appointed a Data Protection Officer. Contact: dpo@avojotask.com

9. Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach:

  • We will assess the risk to your rights and freedoms
  • We will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware
  • We will notify relevant authorities as required by law
  • We will provide details of the breach and remedial measures

10. Privacy by Design

AVIJO TASK PRO implements Privacy by Design principles:

  • Data minimization: We collect only necessary information
  • Purpose limitation: Data is used only for stated purposes
  • Storage limitation: Data is not retained longer than necessary
  • Integrity and confidentiality: Strong security measures protect data

11. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

GDPR compliance regarding tracking:

  • No third-party tracking cookies
  • No cross-site tracking
  • No analytics that identify individuals
  • No targeted advertising
  • Explicit consent required for any tracking (not used by default)

12. Children's Personal Data

AVIJO TASK PRO does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it promptly and notify parents/guardians where possible.

13. Automated Decision Making

AVIJO TASK PRO does not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects for data subjects.
