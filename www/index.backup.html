About & policies

How AVIJO TASK is made and how it treats your information.

About AVIJO TASK

AVIJO TASK is a personal workspace in one page. It brings together four tools: notes and plans, a calendar, a word-meaning helper, and a music and video player with playlists.

How the app is made
  • It is a single web page built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No account or server is needed.
  • Notes, calendar plans and saved words are kept in your browser and synced to your private account storage when you are signed in.
  • The player reads files you choose and plays them directly in your browser. Files are not uploaded anywhere.
  • A small word list works offline. Online English definitions come from Free Dictionary API; Other selected lookup languages are not supported by the online dictionary yet.
  • The design adapts to phones and computers, and follows light and dark mode.
Privacy policy

We do not sell or share your personal data. Your notes, plans and words are saved in your browser and, when you are signed in to Claude, in your own private account storage so they appear on all your devices. If you request an online English definition, only the word you entered is sent to Free Dictionary API; this lookup is not Claude AI. You can use the offline word list without sending a lookup. We do not use cookies for tracking and we do not run analytics. The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive a standard web request from your browser.

Your data and storage

Because data lives on your device, clearing your browser data, using private browsing, or switching devices will remove or hide it. Use the export button below to keep a backup. In Chrome and Edge, AVIJO TASK can remember the folder you chose and ask you to reconnect with one tap. Other browsers need you to choose files again. AVIJO TASK only reads what you pick, never uploads it, and you can revoke access in your browser’s site settings.

Terms of use

AVIJO TASK is provided as is, without warranties. You are responsible for the content you write and the media you play, and for having the right to use those files. Please do not rely on AVIJO TASK as the only copy of important information.

Media and copyright

Only play files you own or are allowed to use. AVIJO TASK does not provide, host or download music or video.

Accessibility

Controls can be used with a keyboard, focus is clearly shown, text adjusts to light and dark themes, and animation is reduced if your device asks for it.

Contact and updates

Policies may change as the app grows. The latest version always appears on this page. Add your contact email here when you publish the app.
