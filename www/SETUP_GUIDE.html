⚡ Quick Start (5 Minutes)

Step 1: Link CSS & JavaScript

Add these lines to your www/index.html <head> section:

<!-- Professional 4K Design System --> <link rel="stylesheet" href="pro-4k-styles.css"> <!-- Enhanced Video Player & Settings Manager --> <script src="pro-player.js" defer></script>

Step 2: Update Navigation Menu

Add these links to your navigation/footer:

<nav> <a href="privacy-policy.html">Privacy Policy</a> <a href="terms-of-service.html">Terms of Service</a> <a href="accessibility.html">Accessibility</a> <a href="gdpr-compliance.html">GDPR Compliance</a> </nav>

Step 3: Verify Video Player HTML

Ensure these elements exist in your HTML:

<video id="media" playsinline controls></video> <div id="videoNav"> <button id="videoPrev">‹</button> <button id="videoNext">›</button> </div> <div id="videoPlaylist">...</div>

Step 4: Deploy to App Store

  • Update app version number
  • Customize compliance documents with your company info
  • Test on iOS and Android devices
  • Submit to App Store and Google Play
